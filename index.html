常見問題

鬧鐘沒有響？請到「設定 → 08一定叫醒你」確認鬧鐘權限已開啟。本 App 需要 iOS 26 以上。
按了停止，為什麼又響？這是設計好的。系統畫面上的停止只會停掉這一次；要在 App 裡答對數學題、再過記憶按鍵關卡，鬧鐘才會真的停。
響多久才會停？從你設定的時間開始，最多連續響 30 分鐘。過關之後 5 分鐘還會再問一次「還醒著嗎？」。
聲音太小？鬧鐘的音量跟著「設定 → 聲音與觸覺 → 鈴聲與提示」，App 不會、也不能自己調大音量。睡前請把這個音量調大。
可以戴耳機嗎？請不要。警報聲很大，戴耳機可能傷害聽力。
可以只靠它趕飛機、提醒吃藥嗎？請不要只靠一個鬧鐘。手機沒電、被關機或系統延遲時，任何鬧鐘都可能不響。
前一晚想取消鬧鐘？可以，但一樣要先過兩關，免得你睡前一時心軟。
資料會上傳嗎？不會。App 不連網路，所有設定只存在你的裝置裡。詳見隱私權政策。

FAQ

The alarm didn't ring.Check that alarm access is on in Settings → 08一定叫醒你 (the app's name on your Home Screen). The app requires iOS 26 or later.
I tapped Stop. Why is it ringing again?That's by design. The system Stop button only silences one ring. Solve the math problems and the memory challenge in the app to stop it for real.
How long does it ring?Up to 30 minutes from the time you set. Five minutes after you pass, it asks "Still awake?" once more.
It's too quiet.Alarm volume follows Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Ringtone and Alerts. The app can't raise it for you, so turn it up before bed.
Can I use headphones?Please don't. The sounds are very loud and could harm your hearing.
Can I rely on it for a flight or medication?Don't rely on any single alarm. A dead battery, a powered-off phone or a system delay can stop any alarm from ringing.
Can I cancel the alarm the night before?Yes, but you have to pass both rounds first.
Is my data uploaded?No. The app doesn't use the internet, and everything stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
